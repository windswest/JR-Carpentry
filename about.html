About Jim

Jim Rotz has been renovating homes
in Victoria for over 30 years.

No sales rep taking your call. When you hire Jim Rotz, you get Jim. A certified carpenter with 30+ years of experience in Victoria who takes real pride in his work.

  • Certified Carpenter
  • 30+ years working in the CRD
  • Owner-operator. Jim is on every job.
  • Based in Victoria, BC

Jim Rotz has been a fixture in Victoria's renovation scene for over 30 years. Decades of projects across the Capital Regional District, from Saanich to Sidney, Langford to Oak Bay, have given him a depth of experience that's hard to find in a solo operator.

He started in the trade as a carpenter and earned his certification the old-fashioned way: by putting in the time, learning from people who knew their craft, and doing the work until he got it right. That foundation shapes how he approaches every job today.

Jim specializes in kitchens, bathrooms, basements, and general home renovations. He works on every job he takes on, which means you always know who's in your home and who's responsible for the work. There's no foreman relaying messages, no sales people, no surprises.

If you've got a project in mind, Jim will give you a straight answer about what it involves, what it'll cost, and whether it's something he can take on. No runaround.
